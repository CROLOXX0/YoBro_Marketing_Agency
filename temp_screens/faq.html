Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with YoBro Marketing. Clear answers, zero fluff.

How long does it take to see results?

expand_more

For Paid Ads (PPC), you can expect initial data and traffic within the first 48 hours, with optimization taking 2-4 weeks. For organic strategies like SEO and Content Marketing, typical timelines are 3-6 months to see significant compounding growth. We provide weekly performance reports regardless of the channel.

What is your pricing structure?

expand_more

We operate on a transparent, flat-retainer model plus ad-spend. There are no hidden fees or percentage-of-spend models that misalign our incentives. You know exactly what you pay every month. View our Pricing page for detailed tier breakdowns.

Do I need a minimum ad budget?

expand_more

To ensure we can gather enough data to optimize effectively, we recommend a minimum ad spend of $3,000/month for Meta/Google Ads. However, for local service businesses or highly niche B2B, we can sometimes work with lower budgets.

Who owns the accounts and data?

expand_more

You do. 100%. We work inside your Business Managers, Ad Accounts, and Analytics profiles. If we ever part ways, all the campaigns, pixels, data, and creatives remain entirely yours.

How does communication work?

expand_more

We set up a dedicated Slack/WhatsApp channel for your team to ensure direct, rapid communication with your account manager and strategists. You also get a live dashboard available 24/7, and we schedule bi-weekly strategy calls.
